Privacy Policy

Word Scramble — Last updated: September 2026

Thank you for playing Word Scramble. Your privacy matters to us. This page explains what information the app does — and does not — collect.

Information We Collect

Word Scramble does not collect, store, or share any personal information. The app does not require an account, login, email address, or any personal details to play.

Local Game Data

Your game progress (such as current level, coins earned, and hearts) is stored only on your own device. This data is never transmitted to us or to any third party, and is not accessible by anyone else.

Third-Party Services

Word Scramble does not use analytics tools, advertising networks, or any third-party tracking services.

Permissions

The app does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or any other device features beyond what is required to run the game itself.

Children's Privacy

Word Scramble is suitable for a general audience, including children. Since no personal data is collected from anyone, there is no personal information gathered from children either.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is updated in the future, the changes will be posted on this same page with a new "Last updated" date.
